A Guide to the genus Trachymela

An interactive resource to aid in the identification of the species of this large and taxonomically difficult genus of leaf beetles. The guide makes use of the Lucid identification system as the key, allowing character states to be specified in any order.

IDENTIFY (LUCID) SPECIES INDEX INTRODUCTION TAXONOMY USING THE KEY GLOSSARY

The leaf beetle genus Trachymela (Chrysomelidae : Chrysomelinae) consists of a large number of species, characterized by their warty dorsal surface, often cryptic brown-to-black coloration with a dorsal surface covered in a layer of granular wax. This guide aims to resolve the difficulties in identifying these closely related species by utilizing a combination of geographic distribution, host plant associations, and morphological measurements.

Whether you are working with live field specimens or museum collections, start with the Introduction for an overview of the genus or use the Identify button to begin a Lucid identification session.
